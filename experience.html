Largest works by capacity From the register above

The register is dominated by water infrastructure for Surat Municipal Corporation: the upgradation of the Singanpore sewage treatment plant to 255 MLD, pumping stations of 138 MLD (Valak), 55 MLD (Navagam), 52 MLD (Udhana) and 51.80 MLD (Vesu-Bharthana), and reservoirs up to 170 lakh litres underground at Kosad and 27 lakh litres elevated at Dabholi and Ashvinikumar.

Outside Surat's water network, the record includes a 12,500 m³ raw water reservoir at Kakrapar for the Nuclear Power Corporation of India, a 10,370-metre DICL pipeline at Dindoli, a bus station at Bilimora for GSRTC, and RCC road works in Surat, Kadodara and Vadodara.
