The Firm

A government contractor the city has relied on for over twenty-five years

M/s Savalia Jaysukh M. manages the complexity of a construction process from start to finish — creating a positive impact on society and supporting its positive transformation.

The firm builds sewage pumping stations, underground and elevated service reservoirs, institutional buildings, bus stations and RCC roads. Its works connect communities, play a key role in urban and rural development, and provide vital long-term infrastructure for the State Government and the Central Government of India.
