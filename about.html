Who We Are

The Firm

M/s Savalia Jaysukh M. is a government contractor company based in Surat, founded in 1997 — and directed by its founder ever since.

The Beginning

Founded in 1997 by Jaysukhbhai Savalia

The company was founded in the year 1997 by Jaysukhbhai Savalia, who has been directing the company since the company was founded.

We have provided just-in-time support to government by building high quality Sewage Pumping Stations (SPS), Sluice Regulator Structures, Elevated Service Reservoirs (ESR), Underground Service Reservoirs (UGSR), RCC Roads and Institutional Building Projects for the State Government and Central Government of India.

We work closely with clients and partners. Our projects connect communities, play a key role in urban and rural development, help drive economic growth and provide vital long-term infrastructure.

Services

Specialised in RCC structural works

We are specialised in construction of RCC structural works with high quality and uncompromising standards. Our suite of integrated construction services ensures the highest degree of quality, safety, efficiency and innovation on our projects.

Works executed include Sewage Pumping Stations (SPS), Sluice Regulator Structures, Elevated Service Reservoirs (ESR), Underground Service Reservoirs (UGSR), Institutional Building Projects, Sewage Treatment Plants, minor dams, reservoirs, RCC roads, flood gate structures and drainage projects.
